# Provisional synthetic training material

60 authored synthetic training dialogues, with 115 unambiguous role labels. Labels are assistant-authored and not independently human-approved. SFT data is prepared; the language-model weights have NOT been fine-tuned by this checker. The live checker uses TF-IDF retrieval of training lessons plus observable-fact rules. Held-out dialogues are excluded from training memory. Historical benchmark results are in ../../docs/benchmark_report.html; they are not a guarantee on new real data.
